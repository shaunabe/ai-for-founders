01

Skills

From one-off prompts to a reusable, shared library.

Shift 01 · Skills

We stopped re-explaining ourselves every time.

What changed

  • A Skill packages the context, format, and steps for a recurring task — write it once, reuse it forever.
  • We moved our most-repeated work out of ad-hoc chats and into a shared library kept in version control.
  • One guide now onboards the whole team, so a Skill one person builds, everyone gets.

Skills in use today

  • deal-robot — scores and files new deals
  • deal-memo — call notes → draft memo
  • investor-list — builds & scores target lists
  • contact-dedup — cleans the CRM
  • lp-deck-builder — drafts fund decks
  • standup & to-do — weekly ops, automated
What it means for you

Find the task you re-explain to AI every week. Write it down once as a Skill. That's the single highest-return hour you can spend.

02

Robots

From opening a chat to work that runs on its own.

Shift 02 · Robots

The best automation is the work you never have to start.

What changed

  • We moved our core database to a setup AI can read and write directly — retiring most of our old glue-code and Zapier wiring.
  • A dedicated robot inbox turns a forwarded email into action for us — a logged deal, an updated record, a Slack post.
  • It runs in the cloud on a schedule, so nothing depends on a laptop being open.

Running daily, unattended

  • Portfolio-update scanner
  • Call-transcript archiver
  • New-deal intake & auto-dedupe
  • Inbound-form processing
  • Contact sync across ~3,500 records
What it means for you

Stop thinking "what can AI answer?" and start asking "what recurring job can run without me?" Triggers and schedules beat chats.

03

Tools

From raw data and meeting notes to things people can use.

Shift 03 · Tools & artifacts

Your meetings and spreadsheets are raw material.

Data → shareable tools

  • Cap Stack Compass (creditdb.ai) — a chat-based finder for non-dilutive & credit capital, open to founders and co-investors.
  • Intro-list tools — investor and customer target lists you can actually click through and act on.
  • All vibe-coded on top of the same data the robots keep current.

Meetings → artifacts

  • One call transcript now becomes a structured database entry, a pre-memo, and an investor-update draft.
  • The same pipeline produced a quarterly portfolio report spanning all 35 active Fund 3 companies.
What it means for you

Don't let your calls and data sit dead in a folder. The recording is a first draft of your memo, your CRM update, your next tool.
